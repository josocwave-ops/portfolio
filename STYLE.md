# onA — style brief

Ona Cabedo Wells's portfolio: art, ceramics, fashion, writing.
Decided over 14 rounds of rendered references (boards in `public/refs/`, temporary).

## Feel
Soft, modern, a little dreamy. Messy in a good way, never childish or cartoony.
Personal rather than corporate. Light throughout.

## Cover (home page)
- Nothing but the signature: **onA** (small "on", very large "A") with
  *cabedo wells* handwritten underneath, centred, and the four section links below.
- Background: soft white with a blurred colour haze (pink, slate, sage, mauve glows).
- **Swirls**: generated, flowing, tapered strokes (cybersigilism-ish but pastel),
  imperfect: uneven curls, S-bends, open ends, branches, a few thicker spiral ones,
  faint connecting threads, fine dust dots everywhere.
  They grow in from every corner and edge, are **redrawn on every visit**, and
  **react to the mouse** (lean away and brighten near the cursor).
- No objects or photos on the cover.

## Colour
Cool "mineral" pastels plus dusty pinks.

| Use | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Ceramics | sage `#8fa89a` |
| Fashion | dusty rose `#c98fa6` |
| Art | slate blue `#8394b5` |
| Writing | smoky mauve `#a99bb5` |
| Extra swirl colours | pewter teal `#9fb3b8`, rose `#d7a1b8` |
| Ink (text) | `#2a2f3a` |
| Page | `#fcfbfc` + haze |

## Type
- Signature and big handwritten titles: **Nothing You Could Do**.
- Labels, captions, body: **Delius** (cute but easy to read).

## Pages
- **Section pages** (ceramics, fashion, art): tidy-but-wonky rows of white-edged
  prints, each tilted slightly, pale tape in the section colour on some.
  A few swirls at the edges only.
- **Piece pages**: big and calm. Large photos one after another, short caption
  (material, year, a line or two), quiet link to the next piece.
- **Writing** is paired with the work it belongs to (e.g. the memory-cup texts
  sit beside the memory cups), not a separate text-only archive.
- **About**, **Contact**, and a link to the **Clay by Ona** shop (claybyona.com).

## Avoid (all tried and rejected)
Lilac page backgrounds · bubbly/cartoon decals with thick outlines · sticker borders
and drop shadows · paper or graph-grid textures on the cover · perfect geometric
spirals · photo cut-outs scattered as decoration · tarot cards · drawn desk/studio
scenes and "rooms" · a clay piece on the cover.
